# Human Rater Manual: CHAT Validation Pilot

## Study Overview

You are participating in a validation study for **CHAT (Communication in Health Assessment Tool)**, an automated system that uses Large Language Models to assess oral health literacy from patient-doctor conversation transcripts.

Your task is to independently rate a set of transcripts on two key dimensions of oral health literacy.

## What You Will Rate

You will assess transcripts on a **1-4 scale** for two primary outcomes:

### 1. Engagement with Health Information

**What this measures**: How actively the patient engages with health-related discussion - do they provide detailed responses, ask questions, and demonstrate investment in their health?

**Rating Scale**:

- **1 (Poor)**: Brief, minimal responses. One-word answers. No elaboration. Appears disinterested.

  - Example: "Yes." "No." "I don't know."

- **2 (Fair)**: Adequate but minimal responses. Rarely provides detail unless prompted.

  - Example: "I walk sometimes." (when asked about exercise)

- **3 (Good)**: Provides relevant, reasonably detailed answers. Demonstrates understanding.

  - Example: "I walk about 3-4 times a week for 30 minutes."

- **4 (Excellent)**: Detailed, thoughtful responses. Initiates clarifications. Asks relevant questions.
  - Example: "I walk 3-4 times a week for 30 minutes after work. Should I increase the duration or add strength training?"

### 2. Overall Health Literacy

**What this measures**: Does the patient demonstrate understanding of basic health concepts, use appropriate health terminology, and communicate clearly about their health?

**Rating Scale**:

- **1 (Poor)**: Lacks understanding of basic health concepts. Misinterprets medical terms.

  - Example: Confuses "cholesterol" with weight, doesn't understand medication purposes

- **2 (Fair)**: Limited health knowledge. May need terms simplified. Vague descriptions.

  - Example: "I think cholesterol is bad, but I'm not sure what foods have it."

- **3 (Good)**: Basic health understanding. Uses relevant terms appropriately. Clear symptom descriptions.

  - Example: "I'm trying to avoid high-cholesterol foods like fried items and red meat."

- **4 (Excellent)**: Strong health knowledge. Uses medical terminology correctly. Articulate about symptoms/history.
  - Example: "I limit foods high in LDL cholesterol like processed meats, and focus on HDL-boosting items like nuts and avocados."

## Rating Guidelines

### Do:

- ✅ Rate based on the **patient's** communication only (ignore doctor statements)
- ✅ Consider the **overall pattern** across the entire transcript
- ✅ Use the full 1-4 scale (don't cluster around 2-3)
- ✅ Rate each dimension independently
- ✅ Add brief comments if a transcript is particularly challenging

### Don't:

- ❌ Don't let one excellent/poor moment dominate - assess overall quality
- ❌ Don't rate based on the medical condition complexity
- ❌ Don't discuss ratings with other raters until study complete
- ❌ Don't look up or research the transcripts online

## Procedure

### Step 1: Practice Phase (5 transcripts)

1. Rate 5 practice transcripts independently
2. Meet with research team to discuss ratings and calibrate
3. Clarify any questions about the rubric

### Step 2: Main Rating Phase (remaining transcripts)

1. Rate each transcript independently
2. Complete in 1-2 sittings to maintain consistency
3. Save ratings to your assigned rating sheet

### Step 3: Completion

1. Submit completed rating sheet
2. Optionally: Brief debrief meeting to discuss experience

## Time Commitment

- Training: 30 minutes
- Practice phase: 30 minutes
- Main rating: ~2-3 hours (approximately 5-7 minutes per transcript)
- **Total**: 3-4 hours

## Rating Sheet Instructions

You will receive a CSV file with these columns:

- `transcript_id`: Identifier for the transcript
- `source`: Data source (DoVA or Nature)
- `mean_score`: CHAT's average score (for reference only - don't let this influence you)
- `rater_id`: [Enter your assigned ID, e.g., "RATER_01"]
- `engagement_score`: [Your 1-4 rating for Engagement]
- `health_literacy_score`: [Your 1-4 rating for Overall Health Literacy]
- `comments`: [Optional notes]
